# What is Salesforce?

Salesforce is a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform used by businesses to manage:

- Customers
- Sales
- Services
- Marketing
- Business workflows

It helps organizations automate processes and improve customer relationships efficiently.

---

# Why Companies Use Salesforce

Companies use Salesforce because it helps them:
> Store customer data in one place  
> Track sales and opportunities  
> Improve communication  
> Automate business processes  
> Increase productivity  
> Manage workflows efficiently

# Core Salesforce Concepts
---
## Lead
A lead is a person who shows interest in a product or service but is not yet confirmed as a customer.

### Example
A student asking about college admission.

---
## Contact
A contact is a verified individual associated with an organization or business.

### Example
A student whose admission details are verified.

---

## Account
An account represents an organization, institution, or company.

### Example
College / Hospital / Company

---

## Opportunity
An opportunity represents a potential business deal or ongoing process.

### Example
Admission process for a student.

---

# Business Workflow:

Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer

## Workflow Explanation

Step 1 — Lead
A person shows initial interest.
Step 2 — Contact
The person's information gets verified.
Step 3 — Opportunity
A possible business deal/process begins.
Step 4 — Customer
The process is successfully completed.
